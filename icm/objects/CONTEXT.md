# objects — nouns

One job: hold one card per durable noun. Do not slurp this folder.

## Inputs

- Working (this run): the noun named by [../effects/CONTEXT.md](../effects/CONTEXT.md) or [../areas/CONTEXT.md](../areas/CONTEXT.md)
- Reference (every run): [../_meta/schema.md](../_meta/schema.md), [_index.md](_index.md)

Do NOT load: more than one card unless the effect index names a second.

## Process

1. Open `_index.md`. Find the line.
2. Open that one card.
3. Follow `See` to source, not to another essay.

## Outputs

- None. Cards are factory (stable). New noun = copy `../_templates/object.md`.

## Human check

A card marked `verified` has a date, a commit/branch, and a `path:line`. Otherwise mark `stub` or `stale`.
